My name is Cora. I originally studied Marine Biology at Bangor University before completing an MSc in Big Data Biology at Cardiff University, where I developed skills in bioinformatics and computational biology. My research project focused on single-cell transcriptomic analysis of pancreatic cancer, with a particular focus on cell-cell communication inference and cellular state transitions involved in oncogenesis. 

During the DTE, I hope to improve my mechanistic modelling capabilities, aiming to take a data-driven approach, utilising the rapidly growing body of omics data to inform and parameterise models of disease and cellular state transitions. 

Outside academia, I am an avid hiker, climber, and chess player!

I am Gianpiero and I am a PhD student in the second cohort of the Data Driven Engineering and Sciences DTE. Before joining this programme, I was a Senior AI Engineer at a UK Consultancy and I have worked in Data Science and Software Engineering across different industries (Fintech, Energy Trading). I hold an MMath from the University of Warwick.

Research-wise, I am broadly interested in how perception, learning, and agency work in intelligent systems. A non-exhaustive list of topics that are of great interest to me and that I would like to explore during my PhD would include: mechanistic interpretability of foundation models, understanding memorisation vs generalisation in transformers, representation learning in deep neural networks, causal discovery in open-ended worlds, and efficient learning algorithms.

I really like music and you can find me at jams where I play guitar and bass (badly). I love listening to bossa nova and samba music and very unserious podcasts. I dabble a bit in salsa dancing and bouldering.

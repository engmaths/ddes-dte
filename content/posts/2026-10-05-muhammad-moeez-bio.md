I am a first-year PhD student in Data-Driven Engineering and Sciences in the School of Engineering Mathematics and Technology at the University of Bristol. Before this, I completed an MSc in Robotics at Bristol, supported by a Think Big Scholarship. My MSc dissertation focused on building a wearable gait analysis system, using sensor data and deep learning to predict joint angles during walking.

For my PhD I am moving towards data-driven mathematical modelling, with applications across interdisciplinary areas such as neuroscience, structural dynamics and digital health. I am particularly interested in combining physics-based models with machine learning, and I work with JAX and the SciML ecosystem in Julia. I am looking forward to learning from the cohort and building a stronger mathematical foundation for modelling complex systems.

I am currently pursuing a PhD in Data-Driven Engineering and Sciences in the School of Engineering Mathematics and Technology at the University of Bristol. I previously studied MSc Robotics at Bristol, following a bachelor's degree in Automation at Tianjin University. During my master's dissertation, I investigated how leg stiffness affects the locomotion performance and energy efficiency of a hexapod robot through experimental research. I am excited to broaden my skill set by exploring data science, machine learning, and numerical modelling this year, building on what I have previously learned in robotics and control. I am particularly interested in how data-driven approaches and mathematical modelling can be applied to robotics and engineering systems, and hope to further develop my research in these areas over the coming years.

Outside of academia, I enjoy travelling, exploring new places, and spending time with friends.

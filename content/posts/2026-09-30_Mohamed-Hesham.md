Hi, I'm Mohamed. I graduated with a BEng in Computer Science, then completed an MSc in Robotics. My master's project focused on a soft back exoskeleton for posture monitoring and control that users could put on and take off independently. I am now pursuing a PhD in Data-Driven Engineering and Sciences at the University of Bristol. My research interests are in assistive technologies and human augmentation, particularly how sensor fusion and data modelling can generalise device behaviour across users, and I am currently exploring computational neuroscience. Away from research, I play football and padel, and I'm always open to trying new sports.

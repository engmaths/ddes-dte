Hello, my name is Kangjun Ji, and I am a PhD student in Cohort 2 of the DDES DTE programme. I obtained a First Class Honours MEng degree in Biomedical Engineering from Imperial College London, where my research focused on AI4Healthcare, computer vision, generative modelling, and language models. I am passionate about continuing my work in AI4Healthcare, and I aim to translate my research into real-world applications with the potential for commercialisation.

Outside of research, I enjoy going to the gym, hiking, and swimming. I am always happy to connect with others who are interested in AI4Science!
